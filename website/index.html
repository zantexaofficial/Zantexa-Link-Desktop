GET STARTED

From download to connected.

01

Install

Run the installer on your computer, or open the APK on Android and allow installation from this source.

02

Open both

Launch Zantexa Link on your computer and phone. They can discover one another on the same network.

03

Pair once

Compare the pairing codes and approve the connection. Your devices reconnect automatically.

Windows Phone and iOS are not supported. Android features requiring notifications or Accessibility may require the full APK.
